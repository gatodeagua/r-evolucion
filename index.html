01 // IDEOLOGICAL & ARTISTIC FRAMEWORK

MANIFESTO

R-Evolución is a multidisciplinary collective and cultural association dedicated to non-violent artivism, performative arts, and all forms of body, visual, and sonic expression. Grounded in the defense of Human Rights, we use live creation—from movement to experimental sound and live AV—as an act of poetic resistance, turning public spaces and stages into laboratories for social transformation, radical empathy, and collective memory.

From grassroots workshops to international collaborations, we believe art is neither a commodity nor a privilege, but an urgent, democratic, and necessary tool to rewrite the future.

COMMUNIQUÉ No. 01 — AGAINST THE ANESTHESIA OF THE SPECTACLE

1. WE DECLARE WAR ON INDIFFERENCE.

We inhabit a system designed to manufacture obedience, isolation, and passive consumption. Culture has been domesticated, turned into harmless entertainment for those who can afford a ticket. We reject that truce. Born from the convergence of independent creators, educators, and cultural agitators, R-Evolución operates as an open cell of artistic insurgency. We do not ask for a stage; we occupy the crack between art and direct social action.

2. OUR WEAPONS ARE NON-VIOLENT AND LIVE.

We practice poetic terrorism: the deliberate disruption of routine through theater, experimental sound, dance, capoeira, photography, short film, and live audiovisuals. Where borders divide and fear paralyzes, we deploy collective creation in real time. We disarm the spectacle by making the audience an accomplice, not a spectator.

3. EMANCIPATION THROUGH COLLECTIVE PRACTICE.

We exist to arm emerging artists and young people with critical consciousness, voice, and agency. Through grassroots workshops, creative laboratories, and non-formal education, we dismantle the wall between creator and citizen. Every rehearsal, every debate, and every street intervention is a rehearsal for self-determination and community power.

4. OUR NON-NEGOTIABLE FRONT LINES.

We organize, create, and resist across local and European borders under four irrevocable principles:

  • HUMAN RIGHTS AS FRONT-LINE DEFENSE: Dignity is not negotiable. Every action stands against exclusion, censorship, and structural violence.
  • RADICAL EQUALITY & INTERSECTIONALITY: No liberation is complete while patriarchy and inequality persist—neither on the streets nor behind the scenes.
  • ECOLOGICAL URGENCY: There is no art on a dead planet. We practice and demand sustainable, conscious cultural production.
  • TRANSNATIONAL SOLIDARITY: Against the rise of walls and isolationism, we build alliances with collectives, grassroots movements, and institutions across Europe to prove that organized creativity cannot be contained.

5. NOTHING IS WRITTEN IN STONE.

Every hegemonic narrative can be hacked. Every silenced body can reclaim the space.

“Tot està per fer i tot és possible.” (Everything remains to be done and everything is possible.) — Miquel Martí i Pol
